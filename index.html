      How to fold

      Paint the sheet, draw a crease, then swing the paper over it.

      Phone or desktop
      Paint: drag on the sheet. Crease: drag a line; its ends snap to corners, midpoints and crossings. Fold: drag the paper next to a crease and it swings toward you; or tap a flap, then press Valley or Mountain. Drag empty space to orbit.
      VR placement
      Grab the yellow bar at the table's front edge to slide, raise or lower it, and the round knobs to turn it. The bar under the control panel moves the panel. The panel's Place & view page has Place in front of me, Seated, Standing, fine steps, a lock, the surface style and the view.
      VR tools
      Pinch the crease stylus or cutter in the tray to pick it up, then touch the paper with its glowing tip and draw; lift to commit. Pinch next to the tray to put it down, then pinch a flap to fold it.
      VR controllers
      Point and hold the trigger to paint, draw creases or grab a flap. Squeeze the grip to carry the sheet. Use the panel on your left.
      VR hand tracking
      Touch the sheet with your index finger to paint or draw creases. Pinch near a flap and move your hand to fold it. Pinch from a distance to use the ray. Poke panel buttons with a fingertip.
      Cutting
      Cut follows crease lines. Tap a segment between two crossings to cut it, or drag along a crease to cut just part of it. Cuts go through every layer under them. Once a piece is cut free, the Move tool carries that piece on its own.
      Projects (☰)
      The menu at the top left: New starts from a preset, Open shows your saved projects (with pictures), Save keeps the current one in this browser, Import reads a .json composition from a file, and Export saves the composition, the net as SVG, the 3D model (GLB in metres, or OBJ + MTL in millimetres) or a PNG of the view. Unsaved changes are named under the project title; New, Open and Import ask before discarding them. Projects saved by earlier versions are listed too.
      Solid Modeling
      Add / Remove: tap a face to target it, choose cube, tetrahedron, octahedron, cuboctahedron or cylinder and Add on face; only faces of the same shape and size join, and joined faces disappear so the inside stays hollow. The cylinder is a 12-sided prism: its square sides join cubes and cuboctahedron squares, its 12-sided ends join other cylinders. Remove, Duplicate, Rotate module and Tip over act on the targeted module; drag a module to move it, and it snaps to a matching face. Symmetry: tap modules to select them, choose Mirror face and tap a face, then Reflect adds their mirror images across that face's plane; if any copy would overlap or only partly touch a module, nothing is added and the reason is given. Scale: select modules (or All modules) and Enlarge or Reduce; every edge changes by the same factor (20–480 mm), and a group joined to unselected modules is not scaled alone.
      Surface Modeling
      Push and pull the floating form directly: drag a corner or an edge. Tap to select a group of corners (a face selects its corners). Corners that must move to keep every face flat are shown in orange; when a move reaches a limit (a face would fold or turn inside out) it stops and the status line says why. Triangulate a face (Net → Triangulate) to let it fold.
      Net
      Unfold all lays the net flat; Refold all closes it; Reset net puts back the default cuts. Open: tap a panel and swing it on its hinge (Group swings everything hinged beyond it, Single just that panel). Edit edge: each deliberate tap switches an edge between hinge and cut, with a brief flash; Lock keeps an edge as it is. Join edge: as Hinge, tap a cut edge whose two sides meet (or the edge on the floating form) to join it with a fold; as Merge, tap the edge between two panels that are coplanar in the assembled form and they become one panel, if the result is convex (a merge that would close a loop of hinges cuts one hinge on that loop and names it). Triangulate: tap a face; a four-sided face shows its two diagonals and a face with more corners a full split into triangles from one corner (◀ ▶ to switch), and Apply adds the fold lines; Triangulate at corner splits every face at a corner. Arrange: drag pieces and turn them with ⟲ ⟳; Arrange never cuts, and equal numbers mark the edges that join. Alternate nets: Fewest additional cuts keeps your hinges and cuts and finds the fewest extra cuts that separate every overlapping pair (one cut first, then two…), with the reason for each; Other hinge layouts searches different nets. Previews never replace your arrangement until you Accept, and Undo returns to it.
      View
      The toolbar on the right works in every tab: Fit all (F), Focus selected (.), Rotate (I: drag the form to turn it; Alt+drag any time) and Reset view (0); More has quarter turns, and the last button shows or hides the labels. Drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom toward the pointer. The view is framed in the part of the screen no panel covers. Undo and Redo sit above the tools (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z).
      Info and Errors
      ⓘ Info lists the composition (modules, panels, internal faces removed, hinges, cuts). Errors counts the layout issues (folds left, overlaps, warped panels, tilted pieces); open it to see the list, tap an issue to highlight it, then Focus to bring it into view. The SVG export uses the same check.
      VR panel
      The same tabs and names: Solid Modeling, Surface Modeling, Net with its sub-tools, then Undo, Redo, Info, Errors and Focus issue, and a view row (Rotate, Closer, Zoom −/+, Focus selected, Fit all, Reset view). ☰ Project saves, starts new projects and opens saved ones. Magnifying never changes real sizes or exports.
      Layers
      “All” folds every layer on the side you grab. “Top flap” folds only the piece you grab.
