How to fold

Paint the sheet, draw a crease, then swing the paper over it.

Phone or desktop
Paint: drag on the sheet. Crease: drag a line; its ends snap to corners, midpoints and crossings. Fold: drag the paper next to a crease and it swings toward you; or tap a flap, then press Valley or Mountain. Drag empty space to orbit.
VR placement
Grab the yellow bar at the table's front edge to slide, raise or lower it, and the round knobs to turn it. The bar under the control panel moves the panel. The panel's Place & view page has Place in front of me, Seated, Standing, fine steps, a lock, the surface style and the view.
VR tools
Pinch the crease stylus or cutter in the tray to pick it up, then touch the paper with its glowing tip and draw; lift to commit. Pinch next to the tray to put it down, then pinch a flap to fold it.
VR controllers
Point and hold the trigger to paint, draw creases or grab a flap. Squeeze the grip to carry the sheet. Use the panel on your left.
VR hand tracking
Touch the sheet with your index finger to paint or draw creases. Pinch near a flap and move your hand to fold it. Pinch from a distance to use the ray. Poke panel buttons with a fingertip.
Cutting
Cut follows crease lines. Tap a segment between two crossings to cut it, or drag along a crease to cut just part of it. Cuts go through every layer under them. Once a piece is cut free, the Move tool carries that piece on its own.
Solid → Unfold
Switch to Solids in the header. Open: tap a panel to select it; its usable hinges glow green and the one in use is yellow. Drag the panel (or use the slider) to swing it. Group swings everything hinged beyond it; One panel keeps only the chosen hinge. Unfold all lays the default net flat; Refold all closes it. Build: tap a face, choose cube, tetrahedron, octahedron or cuboctahedron, and Add; only faces of the same shape join, and joined faces disappear so the inside stays hollow. Edges: tap an edge to switch hinge and cut; Find net searches for an overlap-free net and Check net tells you honestly whether the current layout is a verified net, overlaps, or could not be resolved. Panels that pass through each other turn red. Inspect: drag the form (or grab it in VR with one hand and turn your wrist, or with both hands) to look at it from any side; Reset orientation puts it back. Layout: the net lies flat on the surface; drag pieces to move them, ⟲ ⟳ to turn them, tap a hinge to cut it apart and tap a cut whose sides meet to join it. Equal numbers mark edges that join. Other nets tries different overlap-free nets before you split one. Export… saves the composition, the net as a full-size SVG, or a PNG of the view. Shape: type a width, height and depth (or use ±10% in VR) to stretch the whole composition along its own axes; Reset shape returns to the regular solids.
Layers
“All” folds every layer on the side you grab. “Top flap” folds only the piece you grab.
